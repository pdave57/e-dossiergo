e-Dossier User Guide
A step-by-step guide to using the e-Dossier School Management Portal — for state education administrators, zonal/LGA officers, school heads, and staff.

1. Getting Started
1.1 Access the Portal
Open your web browser and go to the e-Dossier portal URL provided by your state's Ministry of Education.
You'll land on the Login page.
1.2 Log In
Enter the email address and password given to you by your administrator.
Click Log In.
If this is your first time logging in, you may be prompted to change your temporary password — choose a strong new one and confirm it.
Forgot your password? Use the "Forgot Password" link on the login page, or contact your State Administrator to reset it.

1.3 Understand Your Role
What you can see and do in e-Dossier depends on the role assigned to you (for example: State Administrator, Zonal Officer, Head Teacher, Teacher, Admin Officer). If a menu or action described below doesn't appear for you, it likely isn't part of your role's permissions — contact your administrator if you believe you need access.

2. Finding Your Way Around
Once logged in, you'll see the main Dashboard.

Sidebar (left side) — your main navigation menu, grouped by area: Schools, Personnel, Students, Sessions, Results, Reports, News, Users & Roles.
On a phone or tablet, tap the menu icon (☰) to open the sidebar as a slide-out drawer.
Top bar — shows your name/profile, notifications, and a logout option.
Dashboard statistics cards — quick counts of schools, students, staff, and other key numbers for your state or zone.
3. Setting Up the Administrative Hierarchy
Schools in e-Dossier are organized as State → Zone → LGA → School. State Administrators typically set this up first.

3.1 Create a Zone
In the sidebar, go to Zones.
Click + Add Zone.
Enter the zone name and a short code (e.g., "Zone A").
Click Save.
3.2 Create an LGA (Local Government Area)
Go to LGAs.
Click + Add LGA.
Select the Zone it belongs to.
Enter the LGA name and code (e.g., "AMAC").
Click Save.
3.3 Register a School
Go to Schools.
Click + Add School.
Fill in the school's:
Zone and LGA
Name and code
Category (e.g., Primary, Junior Secondary, Combined)
Ownership (Government/Private)
Address
Click Save. The school now appears in your Schools list, where you can click into it for full details.
4. Academic Sessions and Terms
Before any enrollment or grading can happen, an academic session must exist and be active.

4.1 Create a Session
Go to Sessions.
Click + Add Session, e.g., name it "2024/2025" with start and end dates.
Click Save.
4.2 Activate a Session
Open the session from the list.
Click Activate. Only one session is typically active at a time — this is the session all new records will attach to.
4.3 Add Terms
Inside the session, go to the Terms tab.
Click + Add Term (e.g., "First Term") with start and end dates.
Repeat for each term in the session.
5. Setting Up Levels, Subjects, and Class Arms
5.1 Create a Level (state-wide, e.g., JSS 1, Primary 3)
Go to Levels.
Click + Add Level, enter name, code, and description.
Click Save.
5.2 Create a Subject
Go to Subjects.
Click + Add Subject, enter name and code (e.g., Mathematics / MAT).
Click Save.
5.3 Create a Class Arm (Sub-Level) for a School
Open the relevant School.
Go to its Class Arms tab.
Click + Add Class Arm, choose the Level (e.g., JSS 1), give it a name (e.g., "JSS 1A") and set the seat capacity.
Click Save.
6. Managing Personnel (Staff)
Go to Personnel.
Click + Add Personnel.
Fill in staff details: name, staff ID, gender, contact info, role (Teacher, Head Teacher, Principal, Vice Principal, Admin Officer, Counselor, Librarian, Lab Technician), qualification, and date of employment.
Assign them to a School.
Click Save.
Transferring staff: open the staff member's profile → Transfer → select the new school and effective date. The system keeps a full transfer history for audit purposes.

7. Managing Students
7.1 Register a Student
Go to Students.
Click + Add Student.
Enter the student's personal details.
Click Save — the system generates an enrollment number and serial number.
7.2 Enroll a Student for the Session
Open the student's profile.
Click Enroll.
Choose the School, Class Arm, and active Session.
Click Save.
7.3 Manage Progression
At the end of a session, open each student and set their progression status: Promoted, Repeated, or Graduated, then confirm.

8. Recording Results and Generating Report Cards
8.1 Configure Grading
Go to Grade Configuration.
Set the weighting for CA1, CA2, CA3, and Exam scores (these should add up to 100%).
Choose whether the grading scale applies state-wide or to a specific school.
Click Save.
8.2 Enter Results
Go to Results.
Select the Session, Term, Class Arm, and Subject.
Enter each student's CA and exam scores in the grid.
Click Save Draft to save without finalizing, or Publish once results are final (published results lock and appear on report cards).
8.3 View / Print Report Cards
Go to a student's profile → Report Card tab.
Choose the term.
The report card displays automatically with subject scores, grades, and the student's class position.
Use Print or Export to generate a printable/PDF copy.
9. Reports and Analytics
Go to Reports in the sidebar to access:

Dashboard Statistics — high-level counts across your scope (schools, students, staff).
Zonal Summary Report — per-zone breakdown of schools, teaching staff, students, and pupil-teacher ratios.
Gender Distribution Report — male/female breakdown across schools or the whole state.
Out-of-School Children (OSC) Report — estimated number of school-age children not enrolled, by LGA.
Most reports let you filter by zone, LGA, or school, and export the results.

10. School Risk Recommendations (AI-Powered)
This feature flags schools that may need urgent intervention (staffing shortages, poor facilities, low performance).

Go to Recommendations.
The screen lists schools with a Risk Tier (Critical / High / Medium / Low) and a Risk Score (0–100).
Click a school to see the specific factors driving its score (e.g., Pupil–Teacher Ratio, Teacher Qualification Rate, Classroom Congestion, Sanitation) along with a plain-language recommendation for each.
Use this list to prioritize which schools your team visits or supports first.
11. Predictions
Student Performance Prediction — open a student's profile → Prediction tab to see a forecast of their likely academic performance.
School Performance Prediction — open a school's profile → Prediction tab for a school-level forecast and full report.
12. News and Announcements
Viewing (everyone)
Go to News to browse announcements, filterable by type and date. No login is required for the public news feed if shared externally.

Posting (authorized staff only)
Go to News → + Add News.
Enter a headline, optional sub-headline, the date, the posting authority (e.g., "Ministry of Education"), and type (e.g., Announcement).
Click Publish.
13. Uploading Photos
Personnel photo: open the staff profile → Upload Photo → choose an image file → Save.
Student photo: open the student profile → Upload Photo → choose an image file → Save.
14. Managing Users, Roles & Permissions
(State Administrators only)

Go to Users to view and invite portal users.
Go to Roles to see existing roles or create a new one, choosing which permissions (e.g., students:create, results:publish) it grants.
Open a user's profile → Assign Role → select the role and, if applicable, restrict it to a specific school.
15. Getting Help
If something looks wrong or a screen won't load, try refreshing the page and logging in again first.
If a menu you expect to see is missing, check with your State Administrator — it may be a permissions issue.
For technical issues, contact the development/support team at dev@e-dossier.edu.
